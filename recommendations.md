#  Business Recommendations

## Executive Summary

The analysis of over **903,000 website sessions** identified several opportunities to improve website performance, customer engagement, and revenue generation.

---

# 1. Strengthen Direct Traffic

## Observation

Direct traffic generated the highest revenue among all traffic sources.

## Recommendation

- Continue investing in brand awareness campaigns.
- Improve customer retention initiatives.
- Increase email marketing and repeat visitor engagement.
- Encourage direct website visits through loyalty programs.

---

# 2. Improve Mobile Experience

## Observation

Desktop users generated the majority of website sessions and revenue, while mobile contributed significantly less.

## Recommendation

- Improve mobile website speed.
- Optimize mobile checkout.
- Simplify navigation for smaller screens.
- Continuously test mobile usability.

---

# 3. Focus on High-Performing Countries

## Observation

The United States generated the highest number of sessions and revenue.

## Recommendation

- Allocate additional marketing budget to high-performing regions.
- Create localized campaigns.
- Expand successful campaigns into countries with similar customer behavior.

---

# 4. Increase Website Conversion Rate

## Observation

Although website traffic is high, only a small percentage of sessions convert into purchases.

## Recommendation

- Perform A/B testing on landing pages.
- Improve call-to-action placement.
- Simplify checkout.
- Optimize product pages.

---

# 5. Increase Average Order Value

## Observation

Average Order Value is approximately **$127**.

## Recommendation

- Offer product bundles.
- Introduce cross-selling recommendations.
- Promote premium products.
- Implement free shipping thresholds.

---

# 6. Optimize Marketing Budget

## Observation

Revenue is concentrated among only a few traffic sources.

## Recommendation

- Shift budget toward higher-performing acquisition channels.
- Reduce spending on underperforming channels.
- Continuously monitor campaign ROI.

---

# 7. Improve Customer Retention

## Observation

A large number of visitors do not complete a purchase.

## Recommendation

- Launch remarketing campaigns.
- Implement email automation.
- Offer loyalty rewards.
- Encourage repeat purchases through personalized promotions.

---

# Expected Business Impact

Implementing these recommendations could help:

- Increase conversion rate
- Improve customer retention
- Increase average order value
- Improve marketing ROI
- Generate higher revenue
- Improve customer experience

---

# Conclusion

The dashboard demonstrates how marketing data can be transformed into actionable business insights using SQL, BigQuery, and Looker Studio.

The findings provide a foundation for data-driven decision-making that can support improvements in marketing performance and customer engagement.
